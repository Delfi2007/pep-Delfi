# ACPIA ML Lab — Results

All numbers are generated by `ml/report.py` from the JSON files in this folder.
Benchmark: **PAN 2012 Sexual Predator Identification**, official test set (218,702 users, 254 predators). Thresholds tuned on a group-aware validation split, then frozen.

**Best model: DistilBERT (fine-tuned)** — F0.5 0.8842, precision 0.9655, recall 0.6614 (TP 168, FP 6, FN 86).

## Limitations

- PAN12 predators were talking to adult decoys, in English, in the 2000s. Re-validation on real (sanitised) Kerala case data — including Malayalam and Manglish — is required before any use.
- Threshold tuning rests on 27 validation predators; differences of ~0.02 F0.5 are within noise.
- Synthetic chats are template-generated and far easier than real conversations.
- These models rank material for human review. They do not determine guilt.
